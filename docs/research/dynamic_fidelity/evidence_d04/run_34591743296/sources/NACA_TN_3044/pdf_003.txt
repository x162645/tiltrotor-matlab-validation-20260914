jump take-off. 
A method is proposed for predicting the general motion 
of a helicopter following a rapid collective-pitch increase such as may 
be encountered in a jump take-off for given rates of blade-pitch change 
and for given rotor-angular-velocity time histories. The equations 
yield time histories of thrust, induced velocity, helicopter velocity, 
and helicopter displacement. 
A comparison of the results of this method with the experimental 
results obtained for a rotor mounted on the Langley helicopter test 
tower is presented.
