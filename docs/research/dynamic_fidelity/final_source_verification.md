## Corrections to manuscript-facing wording

1. Use **王冶平，吉洪蕾，康清宇，邓皓轩，王畅** for DOI `10.7527/S1000-6893.2025.31280` (not an abbreviated “王冶平等” entry when exact author verification is required).
2. Use **王冶平，吉洪蕾，周攀，叶毅** for DOI `10.7527/S1000-6893.2024.30705`; bibliographic publication is 2025, volume 46 issue 2, article 130705 even though the DOI identifier contains 2024.
3. Use **师妍，万志强，吴志刚，巩轶男** for the elastic-aircraft ROM paper; the fourth author is not 杨超. The DOI identifier reflects the earlier submission/registration year, while the journal issue is 2023.
4. Keep the NASA sources separated by role: TM-89428/TM-89404 are XV-15 flight-dynamics identification references; TP-2004-212262 is whirl-flutter design/aeroelastic analysis; CR-2016-219086 is wind-tunnel interference CFD. None supplies the full synchronized actual-total-pitch → rotor-load → heave qualification chain required by the manuscript.
5. For all seven sources, phrase “未报告” narrowly as “not found in the cited public record/abstract or the reviewed local pages,” rather than claiming that no data exist anywhere.
